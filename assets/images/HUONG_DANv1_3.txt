HUONG DAN CAP NHAT ANH CUOI DA TOI UU

Ban nay chi gom anh JPG toi uu va giu dung cau truc assets/images cua project.
1. Giai nen tep ZIP.
2. Sao chep cac file trong thu muc assets/images vua giai nen vao thu muc assets/images cua project hien tai. Khi duoc hoi, chon thay the cac file trung ten.
3. Giu nguyen cac tep khac trong project, bao gom ma QR vietqr.png.
4. Commit va push cac file anh len GitHub de GitHub Pages cap nhat.
5. Mo trang, nhan Ctrl + F5 de kiem tra. Neu van thay anh cu tren dien thoai, xoa cache trinh duyet va thu lai.

Tat ca 11 anh goc 3990 x 5990 da duoc resize ve 1279 x 1920, JPEG progressive chat luong 85. Khong crop; giu nguyen ten tep va ty le anh. Tong dung luong giam tu 116.28 MiB xuong 2.59 MiB (97.8%). Anh da nen va resize khong phai ban goc de in anh lon; giu lai anh goc de luu tru.
